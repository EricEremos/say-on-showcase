<h1 align="center">Say-On 사연</h1>

<p align="center">
  <i>Every choice has a story.</i><br>
  A card game for talking. One table, one question, the whole room.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://say-on.vercel.app"><b>Play in your browser →</b></a><br>
  <sub>No install, no sign-up. Best with two or more phones in the same room.</sub>
</p>

<br>

**사연** is the story behind a choice, the kind you tell when someone asks *why*.
**Say on** means *keep talking*.

Say-On puts one small, good question on the table and then gets out of the way. There is no feed, no score and no winner: just cards on a wooden table, dealt to the people sitting around it.

<br>

## A round

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/balance-round.gif" width="300" alt="A Balance round on a phone: three card backs, each showing two objects, are dealt; one is picked; two matching cards open for 메뉴를 고른다면?; a vote lifts one card, then both turn over and the tallies grow.">
</p>

**Someone opens a room** and shares the invite code, or you pick a room from the list on the first page.

**Three cards are dealt.** Whoever's turn it is picks one, and it turns over for everyone at the same moment.

**Then you talk.** In Balance, everyone quietly picks a side. The votes stay hidden until the last person chooses, then both cards turn and the count appears.

## Two decks

### 아이스브레이크

<p align="center">
  <a href="assets/step-3-talk.webp"><img src="assets/sayon-figma-01-final-20261023.png" width="430" alt="Put a story on the table. A Figma campaign card framing the original SayOn Icebreaker question screen: a film print asks about the most recent thing you tried for the first time."></a>

17 open questions, each on a film print with its own photograph and the date it was opened. The turn owner has a few minutes to answer; the host moves on.

### 밸런스 게임

<p align="center">
  <a href="assets/game-balance-choose.webp"><img src="assets/sayon-figma-03-final-20261023.png" width="430" alt="A small choice. A longer conversation. A Figma campaign card framing the original Balance selection screen with three card backs on the table."></a>

60 everyday dilemmas, from *계획 없는 하루가 생겼다면?* to *메뉴를 고른다면?* Each card back shows its two objects, drawn as a small set of 120 illustrations.

## Talking, without leaving the table

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/chat-open.webp" width="260" alt="The chat opened over the question: messages as paper slips, each name in its own colour, and a message field with 보내기.">
</p>

The chat stays closed until you tap **대화**. A small number tells you something new arrived. Open it and the conversation reads like slips of paper passed across the table.

Rooms can have a 4 to 12 digit password set by the host, and a quiet room clears itself away after 15 minutes.
