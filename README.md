<h1 align="center">Say-On 사연</h1>

<p align="center">
  <i>Every choice has a story.</i><br>
  A card game for talking. One table, one question, the whole room.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://say-on.vercel.app"><b>Play in your browser →</b></a><br>
  <sub>No install, no sign-up. Best with two or more phones in the same room.</sub>
</p>

<br>

**사연** is the story behind a choice, the kind you tell when someone asks *why*.
**Say on** means *keep talking*.

Say-On puts one small, good question on the table and then gets out of the way. There is no feed, no score and no winner: just cards on a wooden table, dealt to the people sitting around it.

<br>

## A round

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="42%" align="center"><img src="assets/balance-round.gif" width="300" alt="A Balance round on a phone: three card backs, each showing two objects, are dealt; one is picked; two matching cards open for 메뉴를 고른다면?; a vote lifts one card, then both turn over and the tallies grow."></td>
    <td valign="middle">
      <b>Someone opens a room</b> and shares the invite code, or you pick a room from the list on the first page.<br><br>
      <b>Three cards are dealt.</b> Whoever's turn it is picks one, and it turns over for everyone at the same moment.<br><br>
      <b>Then you talk.</b> In Balance, everyone quietly picks a side. The votes stay hidden until the last person chooses, then both cards turn and the count appears.
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>
